Naturopathic medicine is a distinct system of primary care that blends evidence-based natural therapies with modern medical science. Naturopathic doctors are trained to treat the whole person — body, mind, and spirit — focusing on finding and treating the root cause of illness rather than simply managing symptoms. Our tools include clinical nutrition, herbal medicine, lifestyle counseling, homeopathy, physical medicine, and more.
